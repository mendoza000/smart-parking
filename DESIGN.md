# Smart Parking design

## Confirmed direction
Operate mode. User confirmed: "Sí: plano protagonista y controles en español". A dark navy/graphite working surface, emerald availability, muted coral occupancy. The brief and confirmation pin the visual world; no alternate identity is substituted by the direction seed.

## Composition
A restrained navigation rail, horizontal brand/connection header, four metrics, then a dominant overhead parking plan with a narrower activity/barrier column. The plan is an exact four-bay diagram, not decorative imagery. Streets, lane arrows and separate entry/exit explicitly relate the browser to the physical model. No invented charts, trends or occupancy history. Empty hardware state remains visibly unknown.

## Visual grammar
Inter Variable locally served. Navy ground #0b111a, graphite panel #121b27, lighter map asphalt #17212e, hairline #283443; white #edf2f8, secondary #a4b1c2; emerald #57d9aa; coral #f18c8e; amber #e7c383 for demonstration. Compact headings, tabular metrics and numbered bays. Quiet corners and flat surfaces rather than glows.

## Signature interaction
A bay fills with its top-view car silhouette on occupancy; a departing vehicle fades/moves out, status and metrics change synchronously. Reduced motion disables movement. Demo controls are the bays themselves, clearly labeled, keyboard accessible. Hardware bays are non-editable and retain last readings on disconnection with an explicit stale label. The entry-gate arm follows only the last Arduino-confirmed software target; commands show pending/error feedback without optimistic movement. The SG90 has no physical position feedback, so UI copy must not imply measured motion.

## Responsive and accessibility
At desktop the plan dominates. Narrow layouts remove the rail, stack the main areas and retain the full map without panning; controls have 44px minimum touch targets. Text and shapes distinguish state beyond hue. Visible focus, reduced-motion support, connection/activity live regions. No external font request, stock image, fake history or unavailable servo represented as real.

## Seed interpretation
Pinned direction takes precedence. Data-art strobing, poster shelves, concealed color-coded labels and paper tickets are declined for product clarity; their useful discipline is precise figure alignment, unmistakable focus, direct labels and stable grouping. The dark working-surface challenger is compatible with the pinned brief but does not introduce editor-like navigation. Map notation contributes consistent legend-to-bay pairing, never terrain decoration.
